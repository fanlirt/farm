Forget boring weekends and predictable road trips! Welcome to Scotland’s favorite transformed farm, happily hidden right in the scenic hills between Edinburgh and Carlisle. We have taken a beautiful historic farm and turned it into the ultimate playground for families, weekend adventurers, and curious travelers.

Our mission is simple: to bring you closer to nature without losing out on modern comforts. Whether you are here to gossip with our furry animals, hunt for cool local treasures in our market, or simply sit back and refuel with the best coffee in the region while the kids rule the playground, we promise an unforgettable day out. Drop by, breathe in the fresh country air, and see how we do farm life differently!
